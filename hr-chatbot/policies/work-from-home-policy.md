# Work From Home Policy

Work from home is a working arrangement. It is not leave and it does not reduce a leave balance.

## Weekly limit

A confirmed employee may work from home for up to two days in a calendar week. The manager agrees the days in advance. Probation employees work from the office unless the manager approves a specific exception.

## Core hours

On a work-from-home day, the employee is available from 11:00 to 16:00 IST and attends scheduled meetings. Work-from-home days follow the normal working-hour expectation.

## What work from home cannot replace

Work from home cannot be used instead of casual leave, sick leave, or earned leave. If the employee will not be working, the correct leave type must be requested. Client-site days and published shutdown days are office or site days, not work-from-home days.
