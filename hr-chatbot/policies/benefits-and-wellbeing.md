# Benefits and Wellbeing

These are company benefit descriptions. Claim processing stays with HR and the insurer. This assistant does not store member IDs, diagnoses, or claim documents.

## Health insurance

Northstar enrolls eligible employees in a group health plan after 30 days of service. Dependents can be added through the HR service desk. Questions about a specific claim, hospital, or diagnosis go to the insurer helpdesk or HR, not to this chat.

## Provident fund and gratuity

Statutory provident fund contributions follow the applicable wage rules. Gratuity follows the Payment of Gratuity Act for eligible service. Payroll questions about a specific deduction are handled by payroll, not by this leave assistant.

## Learning support

Confirmed employees may request reimbursement for one approved course per year, up to the amount published by HR for that year. The request needs manager approval before the employee pays for the course.
