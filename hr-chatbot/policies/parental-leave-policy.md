# Parental Leave Policy

Parental leave follows the track stored on the employee record: maternity, paternity, or none. This assistant does not collect medical details.

## Maternity leave

An employee marked for maternity leave can take up to 182 calendar days, which is 26 weeks. Weekends and company holidays inside the request are included. The employee needs at least 80 days of service before the start date. Confirmation is not required beyond that service rule. Inform HR at least 8 weeks before the start date when the date is known. Shorter notice does not by itself cancel eligibility, but HR still needs the notice as soon as the date is known.

## Paternity leave

An employee marked for paternity leave can take up to 10 working days after confirmation. The 10 days are taken as one continuous request. Weekends and company holidays are not deducted, and the sandwich rule does not apply. Apply at least 7 calendar days in advance.

## Records

Eligibility uses the parental-leave track on the signed-in employee's own record. The assistant cannot look up another employee's parental leave.
