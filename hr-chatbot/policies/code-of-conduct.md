# Code of Conduct

Northstar expects employees to treat colleagues, candidates, and customers with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are misconduct.

## Raising a concern

Concerns can be raised with the manager, the People team, or the confidential ethics mailbox published on the intranet. This assistant is not the channel for complaints, investigations, or disciplinary action.

## Confidential information

Employee records, customer data, and unpublished company information are confidential. The HR assistant can discuss the signed-in employee's own leave record and published policies. It cannot disclose another employee's data.
