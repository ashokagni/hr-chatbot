# Leave Policy

Northstar Labs leave is deducted from the calendar year in which the leave starts. Balances below are the full-year entitlement for a confirmed employee. During probation, casual leave and sick leave accrue at one day for each full 30 days of service, capped at the annual entitlement. Earned leave and optional holidays begin only after confirmation.

## Casual Leave

Confirmed employees receive 8 casual leave days a year. One casual leave request can cover at most 3 working days. Apply at least one calendar day in advance. Emergency casual leave can be requested on the same day and still needs the manager's confirmation. The sandwich rule applies to casual leave.

## Sick Leave

Confirmed employees receive 8 sick leave days a year. Sick leave can be applied on the day it starts, or regularized up to 2 calendar days later. The sandwich rule does not apply, so weekends and company holidays inside a sick-leave request are not deducted. More than 2 consecutive working days need a medical certificate sent to HR. Do not send symptoms, diagnoses, or medical documents to this assistant.

## Earned Leave

Confirmed employees receive 18 earned leave days a year. Earned leave is not available during probation. A request of more than 3 working days needs 7 calendar days of notice. One request can cover at most 15 working days; longer leave needs written approval from HR. The sandwich rule applies.

## Optional Holiday

Confirmed employees receive 2 optional holidays a year. Each request must be exactly one working day. Apply at least one calendar day in advance. The sandwich rule does not apply.

## Sandwich rule

A weekend or company holiday is deducted only when it falls strictly between the first and last working day of the same request. The sandwich rule applies to casual leave and earned leave. It does not apply to sick leave, optional holidays, or paternity leave. Maternity leave is counted in calendar days instead.

Example: earned leave from 22 December 2026 through 2 January 2027 deducts 10 days. The working days are 22, 23, 24, 28, 29, 30, and 31 December (7). The sandwiched days are 25, 26, and 27 December (3). 1 January 2027 and 2 January 2027 come after the last working day in the request, so they are not deducted. The same dates booked as sick leave deduct 7 days because sick leave has no sandwich rule.

## Pending leave

Pending requests are already held against the remaining balance. Remaining days are entitled days minus used days minus pending days.
