# Holiday and Attendance

Northstar's holiday dates live in the HR database. The assistant should use the holiday tool for a current list. The dates below match that database.

## Company holidays

- 2026-01-01 New Year
- 2026-01-26 Republic Day
- 2026-03-04 Holi
- 2026-05-01 Labour Day
- 2026-08-15 Independence Day
- 2026-10-02 Gandhi Jayanti
- 2026-10-20 Dussehra
- 2026-11-09 Diwali
- 2026-12-25 Christmas
- 2027-01-01 New Year
- 2027-01-26 Republic Day

A holiday that falls on a Saturday or Sunday is still a company holiday, but it is not deducted twice when leave is calculated.

## Attendance

The standard week is Monday to Friday. A weekend is not a working day. Leave calculation uses this week together with the company holiday list and the sandwich rule in the leave policy.
